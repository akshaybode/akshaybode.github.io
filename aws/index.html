What the exam actually tests

DEA-C01 is scenario-based: most questions describe a pipeline and ask for the best service or the most cost-effective option, not for a definition. The four domains are weighted like this.

Domain 1 — Data Ingestion and Transformation 34%
Domain 2 — Data Store Management 26%
Domain 3 — Data Operations and Support 22%
Domain 4 — Data Security and Governance 18%
FactDetail
Questions65 — multiple choice and multiple response (50 scored, 15 unscored)
Time130 minutes
Passing score720 out of 1000, scaled. Aim for 850+ on practice tests
Cost150 USD, at a test centre or online proctored
Validity3 years
Out of scopeBuilding ML models, language-specific syntax, drawing business conclusions
How to read a DEA-C01 question. Find the constraint first — least operational overhead, lowest cost, real time, near real time, no code. That one phrase usually eliminates three of the four answers before you consider the services at all.

How these notes were built

The course deck AllSlides_v3.4 (Data Engineer) is 705 slides. Every slide was read, 575 of them were rendered as images, and each one sits in the section that teaches it, next to prose that says what it means and why the exam cares. Section dividers and title cards were dropped; nothing else was.

SourceWhat came from it
Course curriculumThe 21 sections and their lecture lists, so notes and videos line up one to one
Course deck (705 slides)Every diagram, comparison table and bullet, rewritten as prose and real HTML tables with the slide alongside
AWS documentationCurrent limits, quotas and pricing units, because slide decks age — anything corrected is flagged in the notes
Operational practiceThe "Deeper detail" block on every section: quota tables, billing meters, traps, troubleshooting playbooks and production patterns the course never covers
DEA-C01 exam guideDomain weightings, and the Extras page for in-scope topics the course does not cover
Everything works offline. No fonts, scripts or images are loaded from the internet. Open index.html from disk and search, code highlighting, the Explain panels and progress tracking all work.
