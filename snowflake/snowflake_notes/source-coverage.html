Facts corrected against current documentation

The course was last refreshed by its author in May 2024, with the COF-C03 lectures added later. These points have changed since the videos were recorded.

TopicCurrent behaviourWhere it matters
Resource monitors Count credits for assigned user-managed warehouses — compute plus the related cloud-services usage. They do not cap serverless, storage or AI/Cortex spend; that needs a budget. 09, Extras
Gen2 warehouse sizes Standard Gen2 supports XS–4XL. 5XL and 6XL require Gen1. 15
AI_PARSE_DOCUMENT limits 100 MB per file, up to 2,000 pages (April 2026). 14
Exam blueprint COF-C03 uses five weighted domains (31 / 21 / 20 / 18 / 10), replacing the six-domain COF-C02 structure. 100 questions, 115 minutes, 750/1000 to pass. 16
SHOW GRANTS SHOW GRANTS TO USER your_username takes a literal identifier; CURRENT_USER() is not valid in that position. 10
PUT and GET Still unavailable in Snowsight SQL worksheets. Use SnowSQL, snow stage copy in the Snowflake CLI, or the Snowsight stage upload UI. 04
