Preparing for an interview, not just the exam. Every section page ends with interview questions and hands-on practice tasks, and Real-World Scenarios collects the 93 open-ended questions that come up in Snowflake interviews — slow queries, credit overruns, broken pipelines, dropped tables and design trade-offs. The COF-C03 exam is 100 questions in 115 minutes, scored out of 1000 with 750 to pass. The course does not follow the blueprint order, so use this to revise by domain.
